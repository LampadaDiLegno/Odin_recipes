Pesto alla Genovese

A staple in italian culture coming from Genova, its a mix of various earthly flavours that is perfect to go with your pasta or many other recipes

Ingredients

  • Pine nuts
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Pecorino cheese
  • Basil
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Toast the pine nuts in a dry pan on medium-low heat until golden and fragrant, then let them cool.
  2. Crush the garlic, pine nuts salt in a mortar until finely crushed.
  3. Add the clean, dry basil leaves and crush a few more times until combined.
  4. Slowly add the oil and keep crushing!
  5. Add both cheeses and, once again, CRUSH until finely mixed.
  6. Voila', the pesto is ready and should rest for a few hours before using it in other recipes, Enjoy!
