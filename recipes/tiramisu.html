Tiramisu

The best of the best from the italian dessert scene, simple, delicious and extremely creamy, an easy and fast recipe all the family will love

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Mascarpone cheese
  • Savoiardi biscuits
  • Black coffee
  • Sugar

Steps

  1. You need to open the eggs and divide the yolk from the albumen and keep them.
  2. You need to add half of the sugar to your yolk and mix it until it whitens.
  3. You then add the mascarpone cheese to it and mix it again until its a smooth cream.
  4. You should now mix the albumen and the other half of the sugar until stiff peaks form.
  5. Now you GENTLY add the albumen to the mascarpone cream little by little and for each add you mix.
  6. You now have your cream, prepare a tray or somewhere you want to conserve your tiramisu and place a thin very thin layer of mascarpone cream.
  7. Take the savoiardi and soak them into the black coffee note: the biscuits should not be too soaked or they will have bad consistency or straight up break in your hands and add them to the tray.
  8. Now add a layer with around 1/3 of the cream you have left and place another layer of savoiardi on top.
  9. Finally you add the last layer of cream that should be 2/3 left from the last step and you let the tiramisu rest in the fridge for 24 hours.
  10. You made it, now you and your family can Enjoy this fantastic recipe!
